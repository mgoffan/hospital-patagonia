# Assets generados

## Textura `painted-grain.webp`

- Ubicación pública: `public/textures/painted-grain.webp`.
- Tamaño de entrega: `512 × 512`, WebP, aproximadamente 10 KB.
- Generación: herramienta integrada de ImageGen.
- Uso: mapa neutro multiplicado por los colores de paredes, pisos y mobiliario.
- Objetivo: romper la uniformidad de las primitivas sin copiar las fotografías originales ni cargar texturas pesadas.

### Prompt final

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless texture for a WebGL low-poly hospital game. Create a perfectly tileable square hand-painted surface texture that can be color-tinted in Three.js and reused on plaster walls, painted furniture, and clinical floors. Polished late-1990s console game material, subtle watercolor and gouache grain, gently uneven pigment, refined rather than primitive. Orthographic flat material swatch, uniform neutral light-gray base, texture fills the entire square, seamless on every edge. No directional lighting, shadows or highlights. Fine plaster tooth, faint paper fiber, restrained low-frequency variation, very subtle speckle. No objects, borders, lines, grid, text, logo, watermark, perspective, strong contrast, cracks, stains, photorealistic dirt or obvious repeating motifs.

La salida original permanece en el directorio administrado por Codex; el repositorio contiene únicamente la versión optimizada utilizada por el juego.
